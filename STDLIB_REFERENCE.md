## What this covers, and what it does not

This is the **stable** surface: the functions covered by the
compatibility contract in `compatibility.json`, which promises no
removal and no signature change without a deprecation cycle. Those
promises are what make this list worth writing down.

The engine's builtin table contains several hundred additional names
that resolve and run today - aliases, platform helpers, and areas still
moving. They are usable, but nothing here guarantees they will keep
their name or shape, so they are deliberately absent. **A function's
absence from this document means it is uncontracted, not that it is
missing from the engine.**
